## 1. Why a discovery phase
- Right now the idea is not clear enough to give a fair fixed price.
- In discovery we find out **what to build first**, **what it will cost**, and **what can wait**.
- At the end, **you decide**: continue with us, change the plan, or stop. You keep everything we produce.
